# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers and makers who find Webber's work or writing, usually through a blog post, a project or a
shared link, and want to know who is behind it and what else is worth their time.

## Product Purpose

Takken.io is Webber's personal website. It shares his notes, blog posts, mindset concepts, visuals,
tools, gists and projects. Success is a visitor who understands who Webber is and goes on to read,
use or follow something he made.

## Positioning

The site is driven by curiosity: get to the essence of a subject and put it simply, in writing and,
more and more, in visual metaphors. Everything on it is written and made by hand, and the projects
are free to use.

## Operating Context

- Built with Docusaurus; the home page is a React page inside the shared site layout and navbar.
- Visitors arrive from search, social links and project pages, on desktop and phone.
- Light and dark colour modes are both first class.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Projects linked from the home page:
  - **Minis.gg** (https://minis.gg): free games.
  - **docs.nu** (https://docs.nu): free documents.
  - **GameCI** (https://game.ci): open source community.
  - **citymap** (https://webbertakken.github.io/citymap/): free code visualiser.
- The home page keeps a "Latest posts" list, updated whenever a blog post is added.
- Contact and social links: email, X, GitHub, LinkedIn, Stack Overflow, Twitch.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Takken.io. Person: Webber (Webber Takken), a techie from Holland.
- Voice: British English, sentence case, short sentences, first person, plain words (see
  `~/WRITING_STYLE.md`).
- The home page tells a story about Webber as a curious learner: getting to the essence of things
  and sharing them simply. It is not a list of adjectives about him.
- A few personal details (hobbies, likes, influences, books, aspirations) are woven into that story;
  the rest stay off the page.

## Evidence on Hand

- Portrait: `src/assets/webber.jpg`.
- Personal details already published on the incumbent home page (hobbies: coding, skiing, scuba
  diving, running, ukulele; likes such as ferries, audiobooks, multiplayer games, conversation,
  learning; influences; books; aspirations such as becoming a full-time open sourcerer and
  publishing a PC game on Steam).
- No testimonials, client logos, metrics or press exist; never invent them.

## Product Principles

- Essence over volume: say less, mean more.
- Show the work and the writing; let them carry the claims.
- Personal, not confessional: warmth through story, not through lists of traits.
- Free and open by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast in both colour modes and
zero layout shift.
