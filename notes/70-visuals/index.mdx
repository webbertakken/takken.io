# Visuals

I'm always curious and looking to learn. Whatever the subject, I like to get to its essence and put
it simply. That's how I write, and here I'm starting to do the same with pictures: **visual
metaphors** that hold an idea in a single glance.

## Iceberg of disconnect

Many social organisations are divided these days. Think world politics, governments and companies,
but also families and groups of friends.

What shows above the waterline is **social burnout**. Below it sit the quieter causes that hold it
up.
